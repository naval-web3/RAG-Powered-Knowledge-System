Project Aurora - Internal Engineering Notes

Project Aurora is a next-generation battery management system developed by the
Helios Energy team. The system uses a proprietary cell-balancing algorithm
called QuantumBalance, which extends battery lifespan by approximately 37 percent
compared to conventional approaches.

The lead engineer on Project Aurora is Dr. Mira Castellano. The project started
in March 2023 and reached its first production milestone in November 2024.

Key technical specifications:
- Maximum cell count: 512 cells per pack
- Operating temperature range: minus 20 to plus 60 degrees Celsius
- Communication protocol: CAN bus at 1 Mbps
- Firmware language: Rust, with a safety-certified subset

The most common failure mode observed during testing was thermal runaway caused
by a faulty temperature sensor on cell module 7. This was resolved in firmware
version 2.4.1 by adding a redundant sensor cross-check.
